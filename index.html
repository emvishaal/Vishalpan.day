Selected Builds

Automating the mundane.

Each of these started as a complaint about a recurring task. None of them required a budget — just an afternoon and a reason to stop doing it manually.

01

Automated Payslip Engine

Google Apps Script

Monthly payslip generation and distribution was eating days of admin time. I built a script that generates, personalises, and emails every payslip automatically.

Deployed for 400–500 employees. Monthly overhead: days → minutes.

02

Bulk-Mailer Infrastructure

Delivery & Deliverability

Law firms and finance teams needed to send high-volume client and compliance correspondence without landing in spam. I built mailing infrastructure tuned for deliverability at scale.

Reliable mass comms without tripping filters.

03

QR Asset Tracking

Internal Controls

Physical asset records at a branch office were incomplete and unverifiable. I engineered a QR-based tagging and tracking system so every asset could be scanned, verified, and audited.

Deployed at branch level. Tangible audit-control improvement.
